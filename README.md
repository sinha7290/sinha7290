### About

I am a computational and systems biologist working at the intersection of **network medicine, interpretable AI, and multi omic biology**. My research asks how cells decide when to change state, how those decisions go wrong in disease, and how we can predict and reverse them. I lead the PreCSN center at UC San Diego and collaborate with groups across the United States, the United Kingdom, and Europe.

### Research focus

* **Boolean and dynamical network modeling** of cellular decision making
* **Multi omic integration** across transcriptomics, proteomics, and single cell data
* **Network medicine** applied to cancer, immunology, inflammatory bowel disease, and lung fibrosis
* **New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)** for translational relevance scoring
* **Interpretable AI** for mechanism discovery from high dimensional biology
